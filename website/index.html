Messages on your schedule.

XMSGi connects to your Telegram account so you can prepare messages and choose exactly when they are sent.

Built for everyday scheduling
  • Choose chats, groups, and dialogs through Telegram integration.
  • Write a message, add attachments, and send now or schedule it for a chosen date and time.
  • Review delivery outcomes in the sending history.

Security and sessions

Your account, your session, your timing.

XMSGi works with a Telegram user account, not a bot. The session is kept locally on the device so you can sign out without losing access for later.

What stays with you
  • Sign out while keeping the Telegram session available for a later return.
  • Sign out to remove the saved Telegram session from the device.
  • Sensitive local data is protected with Electron safeStorage where the operating system supports it.
